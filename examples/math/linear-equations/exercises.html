Lesson 1: How do you find a missing number?

Open the lesson · Cheat card

🔦 Guided discovery

Forwards first. The taxi charges $3 to start and $2 per kilometre. What does a 5 km ride cost?

🕯️ Hint

5 pieces of $2, then add the fee.

Notice: going forwards you multiply first, then add the fee. Every extra kilometre adds the same $2.

Now backwards. A ride cost $13. Take off the $3 fee. How many dollars were for kilometres?

🕯️ Hint

13 take away 3.

Notice: $10 of kilometre money is 5 km, the same ride as the last question. Going backwards is the forward steps undone in reverse: fee off first, then divide.

🥋 Practice

Solve \( x + 9 = 23 \)

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Take 9 off both sides: \( x = 14 \) why: undo the "+ 9".
  2. Check: \( 14 + 9 = 23 \) why: both sides match.

Solve \( 4x = 52 \)

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Divide both sides by 4: \( x = 13 \) why: \( 4x \) is "4 times \( x \)", and dividing undoes it.
  2. Check: \( 4 \times 13 = 52 \) why: both sides match.

Solve \( 5x - 7 = 38 \)

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Add 7 to both sides: \( 5x = 45 \) why: the "take away 7" happened last, so undo it first.
  2. Divide both sides by 5: \( x = 9 \) why: undo "5 times".
  3. Check: \( 5 \times 9 - 7 = 38 \) why: both sides match.

🛰️ Real-world

A thermostat is set to 50 °F. Fahrenheit is 1.8 times Celsius, plus 32. What is that in Celsius?

🕯️ Hint

Write \( 1.8C + 32 = 50 \). Which number was added last?

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Write it: \( 1.8C + 32 = 50 \) why: Fahrenheit is what we know, Celsius is missing.
  2. Take 32 off both sides: \( 1.8C = 18 \) why: 32 was added last.
  3. Divide by 1.8: \( C = 10 \) why: undo "1.8 times". 10 °C, a cool room.

Your phone was at 100% and loses 4% each hour of map navigation. It's at 28% now. How many hours have you been navigating?

🕯️ Hint

\( 100 - 4h = 28 \). Get the \( h \) part alone first.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Take 100 off both sides: \( -4h = -72 \) why: leave only the hours part on the left.
  2. Divide by \( -4 \): \( h = 18 \) why: minus divided by minus is plus.
  3. Check: \( 100 - 4 \times 18 = 28 \) why: both sides match.

A cloud storage plan costs $12 a month plus $0.05 per GB. This month's bill is $19.50. How many GB are stored?

🕯️ Hint

Same shape as the taxi: a start amount plus a rate times the missing number.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Write it: \( 12 + 0.05g = 19.50 \) why: $12 base, $0.05 for each GB.
  2. Take 12 off both sides: \( 0.05g = 7.50 \) why: the base fee comes off first.
  3. Divide by 0.05: \( g = 150 \) why: undo "0.05 times". 150 GB.

👾 Boss

Gym A: $30 to join, then $15 a month. Gym B: no joining fee, $25 a month. After how many months have you paid the same at both?

🕯️ Hint 1: where to start

Write each gym's total after \( m \) months, and set them equal.

🕯️ Hint 2: the key move

\( 30 + 15m = 25m \). The \( m \) is on both sides: take the smaller part, \( 15m \), off both sides.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Set the totals equal: \( 30 + 15m = 25m \) why: "paid the same" means the two totals balance.
  2. Take \( 15m \) off both sides: \( 30 = 10m \) why: now \( m \) is on one side only.
  3. Divide by 10: \( m = 3 \) why: undo "10 times".
  4. Check: A is \( 30 + 45 = 75 \), B is \( 3 \times 25 = 75 \) why: equal. After 3 months, gym A is cheaper.

Is there a temperature where Celsius and Fahrenheit show the same number? Find it.

🕯️ Hint 1: where to start

Fahrenheit is \( 1.8C + 32 \). You want Fahrenheit to equal Celsius. Call that number \( x \).

🕯️ Hint 2: the key move

\( 1.8x + 32 = x \). Take \( x \) off both sides, then take 32 off both sides.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Write it: \( 1.8x + 32 = x \) why: the same number on both scales.
  2. Take \( x \) off both sides: \( 0.8x + 32 = 0 \) why: gather the \( x \) parts on one side.
  3. Take 32 off both sides: \( 0.8x = -32 \) why: the plain number comes off next.
  4. Divide by 0.8: \( x = -40 \) why: undo "0.8 times". At −40, both scales agree: very cold either way.
