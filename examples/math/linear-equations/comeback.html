The key points (2 min)

An equation is a balance: do the same thing to both sides and it stays true.

3 + 2x = 17
    2x = 14      (took 3 off both sides)
Full explanation: Lesson 1

Undo in reverse order: the last thing done comes off first.

5x - 7 = 38   →  add 7 first  →  5x = 45  →  x = 9
Full explanation: Lesson 1

x on both sides? Take the smaller x part off both sides, then solve as usual.

20 + 0.10m = 5 + 0.25m  →  15 = 0.15m  →  m = 100
Full explanation: Lesson 1, example 4

Always check: put the answer back in.

3 + 2 × 7 = 17   (both sides match)
Full explanation: Lesson 1

👾 hard A ride-share charges $2.50 to start plus $1.50 per km. The ride cost $14.50. How many km?

🕯️ Peek at key point 2

The start fee was added last, so it comes off first.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. \( 2.50 + 1.50k = 14.50 \) why: start fee plus kilometre money.
  2. Take 2.50 off both sides: \( 1.50k = 12 \) why: fee off first.
  3. Divide by 1.50: \( k = 8 \) why: undo "1.50 times".

👾 hard Solve \( 7 + 3x = x + 19 \)

🕯️ Peek at key point 3

Take the smaller \( x \) part, \( x \), off both sides first.

🗝️ Full solution
  1. Take \( x \) off both sides: \( 7 + 2x = 19 \) why: \( x \) on one side only.
  2. Take 7 off both sides: \( 2x = 12 \) why: plain number off next.
  3. Divide by 2: \( x = 6 \) why: check: \( 7 + 18 = 25 = 6 + 19 \).
