1 · How do you find a missing number? 📜 open lesson

  • An equation is a balance: do the same thing to both sides and it stays true.
  • Undo in reverse order: the last thing done comes off first.
  • Usually: take the plain number off, then divide by the number in front of \( x \).
  • \( x \) on both sides? Take the smaller \( x \) part off both sides first.
  • Always check: put the answer back in.
\( a + bx = c \;\Rightarrow\; x = \dfrac{c - a}{b} \)

Glossary

variable
The missing number, usually written \( x \). In the taxi: the distance.
equation
A sentence saying two amounts are equal, like \( 3 + 2x = 17 \).
solving
Undoing step by step, to both sides, until only the variable is left.
